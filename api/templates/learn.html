How this lesson works

  1. Warm-up guess: answer 3 questions before you learn. Getting them wrong is fine – guessing first makes the lesson stick better.
  2. Small chunks: read one short part at a time.
  3. Recall: after each part, the text hides and you write what you remember. The site then checks it and shows what % of the key points you got, and what you missed. This is the most important step.
  4. Explain it simply: at the end, explain the topic like you're teaching a friend.
  5. Spaced review: {{ n_cards }} flashcards are added to your daily review and come back on the best days so you don't forget.

Tip: put your phone on silent and press the 🍅 timer. After 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break away from the screen – your brain keeps working on the topic while you rest.
